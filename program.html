Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
BSCS • 4 Years

Focuses on the study of concepts and theories, algorithmic foundations, and new developments in computing. Equips students with a strong foundation for designing software and future technologies.

Bachelor of Science in Information System
BSIS • 4 Years

Combines systems administration, server management, and network architecture. Focuses on the utilization of software to plan, install, customize, operate, and manage networks.

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
BSIT • 4 Years

Equips students with the knowledge to design and implement business solutions. Focuses on business process management, data analytics, and organizational computing systems strategy.
