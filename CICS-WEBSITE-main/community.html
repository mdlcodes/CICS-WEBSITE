Student Organizations

  • CNAG-IICS

    The Cisco Networking Academy Gateway provides students with industry-standard networking knowledge and certification opportunities.

  • Computer Science Society

    The official student body of CS majors, focused on algorithmic competitions, software development seminars, and tech community building.

  • Information Systems Society

    A professional student organization dedicated to bridging business strategies with innovative technological solutions and data analytics.

  • Pax Romana – IICS

    The central religious organization of the college, focused on spiritual formation, community outreach, and instilling Thomasian core values.

  • SITE

    The Society of Information Technology Enthusiasts fosters innovation through practical workshops, networking events, and tech exhibits.
